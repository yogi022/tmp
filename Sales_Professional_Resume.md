## CUSTOMIZATION TIPS FOR SALES PROFESSIONALS:

1. **Quantify Everything:** Use specific numbers, percentages, and dollar amounts to demonstrate impact
2. **Lead with Results:** Highlight revenue generation, quota achievement, and growth metrics
3. **Include Industry Keywords:** Use terminology relevant to your industry (SaaS, FMCG, Real Estate, Insurance, etc.)
4. **Tell Your Sales Story:** Structure experience chronologically with clear progression and increasing responsibility
5. **Highlight CRM Expertise:** Mention specific tools and platforms you're proficient in
6. **Show Team Impact:** Include mentoring and leadership experience if applicable
7. **Customize for Role:** Adjust focus based on B2B vs. B2C, enterprise vs. SMB, inside vs. field sales
8. **Use Action Verbs:** Generated, Closed, Negotiated, Exceeded, Built, Expanded, Acquired, Retained
9. **One Page Rule:** Keep to 1 page for brevity; 2 pages maximum for experienced professionals
10. **Proofread Thoroughly:** Ensure error-free document (critical for customer-facing professionals)
